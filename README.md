### 📌 About Sanad (من نحن)
<b>Sanad</b> is a Saudi digital bank designed to be a true partner in the user's financial journey. Beyond mere money management, Sanad uses artificial intelligence to understand user needs, transforming financial aspirations into structured, achievable daily actions. 

> 🥈 **Achieved 2nd Place at Alinma Madarik Bootcamp** for innovative smart banking solutions.

---

### 🌟 Key Features & Core Services (خدمات سند الأساسية)

* 🤖 **Sanad AI Assistant (سند يفهمك):** AI-driven assistant that analyzes spending behavior, calculates dynamic income/expense streams, and offers actionable financial tips.
* 🎯 **Smart Goal Setting (تحديد الهدف):** Gamified savings targets designed around user milestones and automated monthly allocation.
* 💡 **Smart Savings & Alerts (تنبيه الذكاء & الادخار الذكي):** Proactively notifies users when approaching critical budgets to maintain financial safety nets.
* 🔥 **Sanad Streak (سند ينمّيك):** Rewards consistent saving habits with cashback, progression badges, and financial growth incentives.
* ♿ **Inclusive Accessibility (دعم ذوي الاحتياجات الخاصة):** Tailored accessible interface featuring voice assistance and screen-reader optimization for visually and hearing-impaired users.
